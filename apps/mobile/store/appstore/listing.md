# App Store listing — Geardown

## English (primary)

**Name (30):** Geardown
**Subtitle (30):** Pilot logbook EASA FAA CASA
**Keywords (100):** flight log,hours,ftl,roster,airline,atpl,cpl,ppl,aviation,crew,captain,first officer,instructor,8710
**Promotional text (170):** The pilot logbook that does the paperwork: EASA, FAA and CASA layouts, airline imports, roster sync and signed flights. Works offline, synced with geardown.app.

**Description:**

Geardown is the electronic logbook for airline, business and private pilots, students and instructors. Log a flight in seconds, keep your totals right, and be ready for your next check, interview or licence application.

LOG FASTER
• Type the registration: Geardown fills in the aircraft, airports and times it can find
• "I'm flying now": stamp off-block, take-off, landing and on-block in one tap, with the airport found by GPS
• Works offline, in flight or on a layover, and syncs with geardown.app when you're back online

IMPORT EVERYTHING
• Airline statements (Air France EP4 and EP5 read directly), exports from logbook and crew apps (LogTen, Leon…), CSV and Excel
• Photos of your paper logbook pages
• Every flight is shown for review before it is saved

A LOGBOOK THAT CHECKS ITSELF
• Automatic audit: overlaps, duplicates, inconsistent times, night time above total, broken continuity, each with its reason
• Recency: 3 take-offs and landings in 90 days, SEP / MEP revalidation by experience
• Licences, medical and ratings with their expiry dates and reminders

EXPORTS AND FAA TOOLS
• PDF exports in EASA, FAA, CASA, UK CAA and Transport Canada layouts, plus CSV and Excel
• Digital flight signature by your instructor or captain, on screen or by link
• The 96 sample endorsements of FAA AC 61-65K, signed on screen or by link, and the Form 8710-1 table worked out from your logbook

FOR AIRLINE AND BUSINESS PILOTS (MAX PLAN)
• Roster from your calendar link (iCloud, Google, Outlook, crew portals) or an .ics / PDF file
• Indicative rest and flight time limitations (EASA ORO.FTL, EU-OPS Subpart Q): an aid, not a replacement for your operator's tools
• Layovers: METAR and TAF, local time, hotel, contacts and the best places around
• Live radar and the ADS-B track of your flights
• Pilot job offers matched against your logbook, and a CV built from your hours

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
Export your whole logbook at any time. No advertising.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Geardown offers the Basic and Max plans, monthly or yearly, with a free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings.

Geardown est le carnet de vol électronique des pilotes de ligne, d'affaires et privés, des élèves et des instructeurs. Saisissez un vol en quelques secondes, gardez des totaux justes et soyez prêt pour votre prochain contrôle, entretien ou dossier de licence.

SAISIR PLUS VITE
• Tapez l'immatriculation : Geardown remplit l'avion, les aéroports et les heures qu'il trouve

TOUT IMPORTER
• Relevés de compagnie (Air France EP4 et EP5 lus directement), exports d'apps de carnet et d'équipage (LogTen, Leon…), CSV et Excel

UN CARNET QUI SE VÉRIFIE
• Audit automatique : chevauchements, doublons, heures incohérentes, nuit supérieure au total, continuité rompue, chacun avec sa raison
• Expérience récente : 3 décollages et atterrissages en 90 jours, prorogation SEP / MEP par l'expérience
• Licences, médical et qualifications avec leurs échéances et rappels

EXPORTS ET OUTILS FAA
