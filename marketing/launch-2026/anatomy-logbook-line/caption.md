## LinkedIn

One line of your logbook has 13 columns. Each one has a rule, and most logbook errors come from two of them: block time vs air time, and simulator time added to total time.

We drew the EASA logbook line (AMC1 FCL.050) like an engineering sheet, column by column:
→ 1–4: date, places and times in UTC, the aircraft
→ 5–7: single-pilot SE / ME, multi-pilot, total time of flight
→ 8–9: who was PIC, landings day and night
→ 10–11: night and IFR, your function on board
→ 12–13: simulator sessions and remarks
